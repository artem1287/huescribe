Privacy policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

The short version.

  • Huescribe works inside your browser. You sign in once with your Google account; your name, email and photo are kept in Chrome on your device. There are no analytics and no Huescribe server, so we never receive your profile, the pages you look at, the parts you pick or the prompts you make.
  • What the extension keeps (your list, your settings and your own instructions) stays in Chrome on your device.
  • This website sets no cookies and loads no trackers, ads or third-party scripts.
  • We don’t sell or share personal data.

Who we are

This policy covers the Huescribe Chrome extension (the “extension”) and this website (the “site”). “We” and “us” mean the people who make Huescribe. Questions about it? Reach us through the Support tab on Huescribe’s Chrome Web Store page.

The extension

Signing in with Google

To use the extension you sign in with your Google account, through Chrome’s own Google sign-in window. Google then gives the extension your basic profile: your Google account ID, name, email address (and whether it’s verified) and profile photo. The extension asks for nothing else from Google (no access to Gmail, Drive or other data). It reads this profile once when you sign in and again now and then to check the sign-in is still valid, and keeps it in Chrome’s local storage on your device. It isn’t sent to us or anyone else. Google’s privacy policy covers the sign-in itself. When you sign out (in the ⋯ menu), the profile is deleted from your device and the extension gives its Google access back.

What it reads, and when

The extension only reads a page when you open the Huescribe panel on it and pick something. Then it reads, on your device:

  • The part you pick: its structure, sizes, spacing, fonts, colors and how it moves (including how it reacts when you hover or press it, if you turn on Motion), plus the text shown inside it, which is used to name the part and recognize what it is (a button label, a heading).
  • The page’s style files: the CSS files that page uses, downloaded straight from the site that serves them, without your cookies, so the description is accurate.
  • A screenshot of the visible part of the tab, cropped to what you picked, so the panel can show a small picture of it (and, if you choose, put that picture on your clipboard).

It doesn’t read other tabs, your browsing history, your bookmarks or your cookies, and it reads nothing on a page until you pick something there. Avoid picking parts that show private information; if you do, it stays on your device like everything else.

What it sends, and to whom

Nothing to us. The extension has no Huescribe server and no analytics or crash reporting. Its only network requests are the style-file downloads described above, sent to the site you’re on, and the requests to Google that sign you in, check the sign-in and, when you sign out, give the access back.

Chrome Web Store user data commitments

The extension’s use of data is limited to describing the design you pick. We don’t sell user data, don’t transfer it to third parties, don’t use it for purposes unrelated to that single purpose, and don’t use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

This website

The site is a set of plain pages. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics, ads, tracking pixels, embedded social media or third-party fonts or scripts. Its fonts and images are served from the site itself.

Like any website, the company that hosts it may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time) to run the service and protect it from abuse. We use them only for that, if at all.

The “Add Huescribe to Chrome” buttons lead to the Chrome Web Store, which is run by Google under its own privacy policy. If you write to us, we use your message only to reply.

Children

Huescribe isn’t directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices and rights

Because the extension keeps its data on your device, you control it: clear your list in the panel, clear the extension’s data in Chrome, or remove the extension to delete everything it stored. Your Google profile is kept only on your device too: signing out deletes it. We hold no account or profile about you on any server to access, correct or delete. If you’ve written to us and want that message deleted, just ask. Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, the UK or California), you may have further rights under privacy law; contact us and we’ll help.

Changes

If we change how Huescribe handles data, we’ll update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect. A change that collects new kinds of data would also come with a notice in the extension.

Contact
