Frequently asked questions

What does PromptLift do?

PromptLift is a Chrome extension. You click a part of any website you like (a section, a button, an animation) and it writes that design up as one ready-to-paste prompt for your AI app, such as Claude Code, Cursor, ChatGPT, Lovable, v0 or Bolt, so the app can bring it into your own site.

Is it okay to copy another site’s design?

PromptLift is made for borrowing ideas, not cloning pages: it describes layout, spacing, type sizes and movement, always shifts colors slightly, and tells your AI app to use your own words, pictures and brand. Leave out other sites’ logos, text and photos. This isn’t legal advice.

Will my site end up looking like a copy?

No. The prompt asks your AI app to keep your fonts, colors and content wherever they fit, and to change only the parts you picked. You choose what to take from each part: colors, fonts, layout or motion.

How is it different from DevTools or a screenshot?

DevTools shows the raw code of one element, and a screenshot only shows pixels. PromptLift reads the part you picked (its structure, spacing, fonts, color relationships and movement) and writes it up as clear instructions your AI app can follow.

Which AI apps does it work with?

It writes prompts for Claude Code, Cursor, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Windsurf, Copilot, Lovable, v0 and Bolt; pick yours at the top of the panel. You need an app that can see or edit your site’s code for the changes to land.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You paste the prompt into your AI app and it makes the changes. If you don’t have a site yet, the same prompt tells it to build one and ask you what the site is about.

Can I mix parts from different websites?

Yes. Your list keeps every part you add, from any site, until you copy it or close the browser. The prompt then asks your AI app to make them look like one site: one button style, one feel for movement.

Can it copy animations and hover effects?

Yes. Turn on Motion to describe how a part moves: timing, easing and what triggers it, including animations that play as the page loads. Movement that’s drawn in code (like a canvas) can’t be copied exactly, so you get a filmstrip and a description to rebuild from.

Will a prompt use up a lot of my AI credits?

A prompt is plain text; after copying, the panel shows how many characters it is. Copy only the details you need (say, just Layout) to keep it short.

Does it work on every website?

It works on most sites. Browser pages such as settings and the Chrome Web Store don’t allow extensions, and a page that was open before PromptLift started may need a reload, which the panel offers.

Where does my data go?

PromptLift runs in your browser. It reads the page you’re on and that page’s style files, then puts the prompt on your clipboard. There’s no account and no PromptLift server, and it switches off when you close the panel.

Which browsers are supported?

Google Chrome. PromptLift opens as a side panel next to the page you’re looking at.
